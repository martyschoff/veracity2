# Scoring implementation (owner-approved): BOTH accuracy+CI AND Brier-with-shrinkage

Owner decision overrides the 'drop Brier' recommendation. Implement BOTH displays:

## 1. Accuracy + Wilson CI (Sonnet's recommendation)
- Per person: % correct of resolved claims (correct/(correct+wrong)), Wilson 95% confidence interval
- Display: "Accuracy 78% (N=108, 71-84%)"

## 2. Brier with shrinkage (owner wants it anyway)
- P_adjusted = 0.7 * P_panel + 0.3 * base_rate, where base_rate = person's overall fraction correct across their resolved claims
- Brier = mean((P_adjusted - outcome)^2) over resolved claims
- Also compute RAW Brier (no shrinkage) for comparison
- Skill = base_rate_brier - brier_adjusted (positive = beats base rate)
- Display: "Brier .21 (shrunk, N=108) | raw .24 | Skill +.03"
- Mark source as 'panel'
- N>=10 to display; N<10 grayed with N shown

## 3. Compute per tracked person: Zeihan, Doomberg, Diamandis, Bremmer, McAlvany (and any person with N>=1; grayed below 10)

## 4. Where: per-person header box in templates/index.html grid columns, under the thumbs. Also render.py computes and passes the stats.

## 5. Calibration: SKIP for now (Sonnet's objection about bucket noise accepted). Revisit when N>300 per person.

## Implementation notes
- Data: data/predictions.json, per claim: judgements[] with verdict/weight, verdict field (correct/wrong/expired), individual_name
- Resolved = verdict in (correct, wrong). Expired excluded.
- P_panel per claim = sum(weight of correct votes)/sum(weight of decisive votes) over judgements with verdict in (correct,wrong)
- Edge cases: claims with 0 decisive votes get excluded from Brier; claims with no judgements excluded
- Render-time computation in render.py (no DB, just compute during render)
- Also compute a global 'panel skill' score for the whole system on the monitor page
