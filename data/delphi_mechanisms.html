1 · What MiroFish set as the standard

  • Pipeline: claim → Graphiti/Neo4j knowledge-graph ingest → specialist agents argue in rounds → ReportAgent verdict
  • Targets: max-context reasoning, full social simulation, explainable rationale trail, graph persistence
  • Cost: high — embeddings + 2-3 rounds per claim, ~15-30 min on local 32B
  • Our move: Delphi3080 keeps the skeleton, re-targets it to daily verdicts at tracker scale

  • Silent pythonw worker → fixture → Graphiti ingest → 2 specialist rounds → verdict + confidence
  • Judge: local qwen3:32b on nimo128 (embed-fixups in the llm proxy)
  • Targets: $0 marginal, always-on, batch all graded predictions, fail-soft queue
  • Verified Oct 7: first clean end-to-end run — nickel 10×-demand REFUTED @0.65 (matches owner mark)
  • Weakness: 32B reasoning ceiling; ingest/embedding fragility (4 fixed today)

3 · DelphiCursor — the Opus tiebreaker

  • Controls: 300s hard timeout, ≤15 claims/day, single-instance lock, schema validation, ≤2 retries
