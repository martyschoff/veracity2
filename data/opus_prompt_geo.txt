We are building veracity2 (this repo): a predictions-vs-reality tracker that extracts dated, testable predictions from commentators' own shows/articles, classifies them (prediction vs prescription vs fact vs conditional via an LLM gate), and renders a QA grid where the owner marks each prediction right/wrong. Panel members also act as adjudicators (LLM-simulated panelist votes, weighted by tier: professional forecasters 1.5x, specialist analysts 1.25x, insiders 1.0x; verdict at weighted score >= 2.0).
