| Name | Role | Reason |
|------|------|--------|
| **Stephen Miller** | White House | Policy advocate. Makes prescriptions ("we should..."), not predictions. |
| **Jamison Greer** | USTR | Trade policy implementer. His "predictions" are pre-announcements. |
| **Scott Bessent** | Treasury | Market positioning. Treasury secretaries don't predict; they manage expectations. |
| **Marco Rubio** | State | Official policy. Classification constraints. Statements are diplomatic signals. |
| **Pete Hegseth** | Defense | Official policy. DoD statements are operational, not predictive. |
| **JD Vance** | VP | Official policy. VP predictions = administration trial balloons. |
